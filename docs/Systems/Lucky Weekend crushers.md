## Rarity and rolling
- New rarity **Limited** in C.Rarities (between Legendary and Secret): `weight = 0`, `eventWeight = 0.02`,
  `event = "LuckyWeekend"`, rank 4.5, price 750,000 and upgrade mult 2.2 (like Secret), colour clover green.
- Rollable only while the LUCKY WEEKEND is on: `C.limitedOn()` = Shared/WeekendConfig `on(os.time())` (the same
  clock as the 2x weekend luck), or in Studio workspace attribute `WeekendTest = "active"`. The server roll gets
  it as `perks.limited`; `C.rollWeights(luck, lucky, bonus, limited)` uses eventWeight then, 0 otherwise.
- Odds at Luck 0, no boosts: 1 in 5,001 rolls for one of the three, 1 in 15,003 per crusher (the Index shows
  "WEEKEND · 1 in N" with your luck). Luck helps it like rank 4.5. Pity (Legendary+) resets on it.
- Willem: as strong as a Secret: `value = C.ChaseValue.Secret` (330) on the Quantum band; row bonus .48.
- Once owned they stay: ownership, equipping, upgrades and production don't check the weekend.
- No big-roll cinematic (that's rank 5+); the roll reveal profile sits between Legendary and Secret.
