# Plan

**Pitch:** you own a hydraulic press. Hold CRUSH to flatten whatever is on
it, get cash, upgrade, and buy a bigger press so bigger things roll in.

**Why:** the press/crush niche on Roblox is almost empty (largest game ~105
players, 19 Oct 2026), the loop is satisfying to watch (clip-friendly), and
it's small enough to build in days.

## Milestones
1. **Core loop** — plots, press, objects, crush, cash, HUD, upgrades, saving. *(done 19 Oct)*
2. **Progression polish** — all 13 tiers looking good, press growth feels great. *(done 19 Oct)*
3. **Juice** — better sounds, debris, screen effects, crush animations per object. *(done 19 Oct)*
4. **Depth** — rebirth (multiplier), golden objects, collection book. *(done 19 Oct)*
5. **Release** — shop + passes (port from WarChest: auto-crush, 2x cash, offline earnings),
   onboarding, analytics, thumbnail and icon.
