PARTS

ChestLid (828): rounded lid including its fixed gold/cyan decoration.

LidGlow (102): moving magenta crystal crown.
Total: 2,864 triangles. No high-resolution source mesh is hidden in this pack.

PALETTE / MATERIALS
Textures/GemChest_Color.png, GemChest_Metalness.png and GemChest_Roughness.png
are shared 256x256 atlas maps, using the same navy/gold/magenta/cyan palette
as the cave props. Keep imported MeshPart.Color white when using the colour
map, so you do not tint gold and cyan purple. There is no normal map needed
for these deliberately faceted low-poly shapes.
There are two material variants in Blender: ordinary and mild emissive.
The preview emission is only a render shader; do not expect that to become
an animated Roblox glow automatically. ChestGlow and LidGlow are isolated
so you may apply a magenta Neon treatment or restrained local lighting and
the existing particles. If replacing their material with plain Neon, their
flat neon colour will intentionally replace the palette shading. The body
and lid should retain their SurfaceAppearance. No particle/light objects,
UI, text or reward logic are included in the FBX/GLB.

SIZE / AXES / PIVOTS
Main body W=8.4, D=5.4; full closed bounds include handles, clasp and crown:
9.28 wide x 7.47 tall x 6.46 deep. Ground is source Z=0.
Root: ground centre of the closed chest, not the bounding-box centre.
SOURCE axes: Blender X right, +Y toward the FRONT, Z up. Front +Y is
intentional, giving Roblox front -Z under the usual conversion below.
Nominal conversion: Blender (x,y,z) -> Roblox (x,z,-y).
Do not apply that conversion twice if the importer already did it.
Check the big front magenta clasp faces the platform at the existing yaw.

Both ChestLid and LidGlow are authored around the SAME rear hinge:
Blender (0,-2.70,4.26); nominal Roblox (0,4.26,2.70).
The root and the stationary meshes are at source ground-centre origin.
FBX/GLB round trips preserve matching lid origins, but Studio may recenter
MeshParts. Restore Model pivots/PivotOffset or use the hinge-relative CFrame
method rather than trusting automatically generated part centres.
Use Model-manifest.json for exact bounds and source pivots.
