The chest has 2,864 triangles TOTAL and four meshes:
ChestBody — stationary hollow body, gold trim, handles and interior gems.
ChestLid — separate rounded lid with gold straps and cyan shoulder jewels.
ChestGlow — stationary magenta front clasp and base light strip.
LidGlow — the magenta crystal crown; moves together with ChestLid.

Colour, roughness and metalness maps are small 256 x 256 shared palette maps,
packed into the Blender source and included separately under Textures.
The FBX embeds colour textures where supported. Use SurfaceAppearance maps
when importing if the importer does not wire them automatically.

Closed size is approximately 9.28 wide x 7.47 high x 6.46 deep nominal studs.
Scale uniformly if necessary. The main body remains 8.4 wide and 5.4 deep,
matching the current chest; the details project slightly beyond it.
The root pivot is at ground centre. Lid and LidGlow share a rear hinge pivot.

This is an actual authored Blender model, not concept art or an AI statue.
